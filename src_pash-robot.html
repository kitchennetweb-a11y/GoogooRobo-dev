Find, Feed and Say it are Farsi games. Just talk to Pash: when he's standing still he listens and repeats it in a funny voice. The Talk button turns his ears on and off. Poke his head, tummy (try 3 times fast!), ears, feet, antenna or face. Drag to spin him. Tap the empty floor to call him home. Grown-ups: Farsi lines show here and will play your recorded voice later.
