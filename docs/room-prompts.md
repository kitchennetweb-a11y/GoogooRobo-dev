# Room painting prompts (Gemini / ChatGPT)

Each room needs **two pictures**: one upright (9:16, for phones held upright) and one sideways (16:9, for sideways phones and iPads).

How to use:
1. Open Gemini or ChatGPT and start a new image chat.
2. Attach a style reference so the new room matches the library and play room: `docs\style-tests\final-library.jpg` (or `final-play.jpg`). Say "Match the style of this picture, but make a different room:" and paste the prompt.
3. Make 3 or 4 versions of each and send Claude your favourites (any format, the biggest size the tool gives). Claude fits them into the game.

Check before sending: no characters or robots in the picture, no writing or logos, the middle of the floor is clear (that's where Googoo stands), and the colours are mid-tone (not pastel, not neon).

---

## Shared style (it's already inside every prompt below)

Soft rounded premium 3D animated-film style children's game background, toy-like world, clean readable shapes, gentle depth, eye-level camera from the middle of the room looking at the back wall. An EMPTY room: no characters, no figures, no robots, no people, no animals, no text, no letters, no logos. Colour palette mid-tone and rich: NOT pastel, NOT neon.
